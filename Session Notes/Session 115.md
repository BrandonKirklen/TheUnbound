# Session 115

**Date:** 13th of Fessuran 
**Days Remaining Until Freya (7th of Quen'pillar):** 23

### Previously On: The Unbound

The party's recent troubles began with **Tomis and Grimbold** setting out to return **Kobach Alanis** safely to his home — a mission that went sideways almost immediately.

Along the way, **Tomis** was ambushed and taken captive by what the party now believes to be **Myriad agents** who had been lying in wait for the return journey. True to his nature, **Grimbold** refused to leave Tomis behind, heroically leaping through a portal after him — destination unknown, though he now suspects it was somewhere on the **eastern coast of Wildemount**.

What followed was a daring, somewhat unhinged mission of espionage and subterfuge. Grimbold managed to extract Tomis before the Myriad could act on their plans, though the rescue came at a cost — **Kobach** did not make it out. His fate remains unresolved, his death another casualty of this ongoing conflict with the Myriad.

The party returned and spent considerable time deliberating retaliation. They knew one of the Myriad agents — a woman previously spotted traveling with their group — and the enemy had demonstrated they could detect and dispel the party's scrying sensors, even issuing threats through them. Despite this intelligence, the idea of teleporting in and taking the fight directly to the Myriad was ultimately weighed against counsel from **the Raven Queen**, who made clear such a course would be unwise.

Frustrated, grieving, and with Kobach's death left unavenged for now, the party steeled themselves and refocused.

Their current objective: travel **southeast to the Alabaster Sierras** to investigate the **Aarakocra situation**. Intelligence suggests the Aarakocra have entered into some kind of relationship or service with a **Nagpa** — one apparently tasked with dealing with *"the upstarts of Tal'Dorei."* Whatever that phrase means exactly, it doesn't bode well.

**Recap & Setup**

The session picked up with the party recapping where they left off — the Raven Queen had advised against a direct assault on the Arakocra, so they pivoted to a reconnaissance-and-bait approach, traveling down the Morin River toward the southern end of the Alabaster Sierras. The party noted the grim side benefit: killing Arakocra may directly strengthen the Raven Queen, which Tomis found deeply motivating.

A running concern for Freya's affliction also surfaced — Pike advised trying a 9th-level Greater Restoration (requiring diamonds worth that spell slot) and offered to help if that fell short. The party noted they're about two levels away from casting it themselves, which Freya's player received with appropriate sarcasm.

**Getting the Boat**

Rather than hire a merchant, the party went through Whitestone's council via a gloriously bureaucratic note-passing exchange with a page they've completely forgotten the name of (Rowan called him Steve). Kaylee — the council's trade official — was too busy to meet in person, so the whole thing was handled in two-sentence notes passed back and forth like middle school. The upshot: Whitestone provided Captain Jethro and the riverboat **the Shale** for a midnight departure, effectively treating it as a military operation.

**Departure**

The party made final preparations — Tomis cast Death Ward on himself, Grimbold squeezed in 7 more hours of spell scribing, and everyone passed their constitution saves against fatigue. Grimbold also excitedly shared his newly scribed **Earthbind** spell — pulled from a slain wizard's spellbook — which chains flying creatures to the ground. Tomis called it the best spell ever, which for him is practically a declaration of love.

---

**The Voyage Begins — The Shale**

The party departed Whitestone at midnight aboard a riverboat called **the Shale**, a modest Whitestone-crested vessel captained by a weathered but younger-than-expected man named **Captain Jethro**. The plan: float downriver toward the Alabaster Sierras to investigate the organized Arakocra presence there.

Grimbold immediately buried his nose in his spellbook. Rowan teleported to the top of the mast and perched there. Grimbold cast **Telepathic Bond** on the group (including the captain), and set up a **Tiny Hut** on the stern, covered with a tarp and ropes to disguise its suspiciously perfect spherical shape as cargo.

The party debated strategy over the bond — Tomis advocated a fog bank approach; Rowan suggested hiding most of the party below deck to bait a smaller attack. They landed on a hybrid plan: look like a mostly empty boat to invite an early ambush, then go stealthy with fog as they neared the mountains. Uriel also planted arrows around the deck using **Cordon of Arrows** (Laserllama edition) as a defensive measure.

Tomis used the quiet time to share with Rowan the history behind his hatred of the Arakocra — decades of raids on his herd, the loss of his son, and years of hunting them through the Parchwood and Timberlands.

---

**The Scout is Spotted**

During watch, Uriel rolled an exceptional Perception check and spotted a **flying creature** regularly cresting the forest canopy alongside the boat — pacing them. Too consistent to be random wildlife.

The party decided to send **Uriel and Juniper** (Rowan's hasted pegasus) to intercept it. Juniper, moving at a ludicrous 180 feet per round under Haste, closed half the distance before the creature even noticed. It turned out to be an **Arakocra scout**, which immediately bolted.

---

**The Chase & Fight**

A running aerial battle ensued:

- Uriel marked the Arakocra as his **quarry** and got hit with a few footbow shots while responding with longbow arrows with it while Juniper closed the gap.
- The scout dropped a **net** on Juniper, restraining her and sending them both into freefall from ~150 feet up.
- Uriel bailed off Juniper mid-fall, teleported upward, engaged the scout in melee, and applied **Ensnaring Strike** to briefly restrain it.
- Juniper broke free of the net (exactly hitting DC 10 on the strength save), rocketed upward, and in a spectacular piece of aerial acrobatics, **caught Uriel mid-fall** — both rolling high enough to execute the maneuver without Juniper taking damage.
- Uriel re-engaged, and a final arrow from the **Bounding Assault** maneuver dropped the scout.

---

**The Aftermath**

Uriel attempted to keep the scout alive for interrogation, but it bled out before a Cure Wounds could land — the initiative order wasn't on their side. He flew the corpse back to the boat and handed it over to Tomis with a dry "I got you a gift." Tomis, whose decades-long vendetta against the Arakocra is well-established, was delighted.

Short and grim summary:

**The Ritual**

Tomis arranged the body on deck, drew glyphs around it with the harpy-claw augury stick, and spoke the words over it plainly — *"You are ended and I will not pretend to grieve. Tonight you will settle and tell me what you know. It's what you owe."* Then he cast Speak with Dead.

**The Problem**

The Arakocra's eyes opened, black and lightless, and it immediately started answering — in Arakocran, which Tomis doesn't speak. Grimbold could have cast Comprehend Languages as a ritual, but it was already too late for that. Tomis burned a Tongues spell on the fly to fix it, eating into the 10-minute window.

**The Responses**

Functionally useless. Every question got a variation on the same answer:

- *Where are the rest based?* → "We are above you in all things. You will die, you will die, you will die."
- *Who leads you?* → "One so powerful you stand no chance. You will die."
- *Are there other scouts?* → "You will die, you will die, you will die."
- *How do you plan to kill us?* → "Painfully."

Tomis released the spell, picked up the corpse, and threw it in the river. Grimbold noted he wanted feathers for the next one. Tomis threw away a use of Tongues for "painfully."

**Reconnaissance**
During the day, Uriel (with Owl's Wisdom giving advantage on Perception) flew Juniper in a sweep around the boat. He discovered several crude elevated platforms in the trees along the river bends — clearly Aarakocra surveillance posts — but found them all **empty and abandoned for roughly a couple of months**, which was encouraging.

**The Mysterious Glint**
Both Rowan and Uriel spotted a recurring **reflective glimmer near the summit of one of the southernmost peaks** in the cluster. The river captain confirmed he'd seen it for years but didn't know what it was. The party speculated it might be some kind of structure built into or against the mountain face — possibly Aarakocra quarters. Rowan noted it to investigate later.

**Enemy Presence**
At around 4 hours out, Uriel rolled a 32 Perception and spotted **large numbers of dark figures moving between multiple mountain peaks** in the cluster — not a small patrol, but something resembling a full settlement or civilization spread across several summits.

**The Plan Going Forward**
- **Wait until ~4 hours from the mountain tip**, then Tomis casts **Control Weather** to generate ground fog for concealment along the river and the approach on foot.
- Keep as few people on deck as needed; let sleeping party members finish their long rests.
- The main concern is **exposure on the mountainside itself** — the river and forest canopy offer cover, but the vertical climb to the Aarakocra settlements will be dangerously open.
- If a Nagpa is encountered: engage and eliminate if it's one, retreat if it's two or more. Freya and Grimbold expressed confidence; Rowan urged caution.

**The Ascent Begins**

Disembarked on the eastern shore of the Parchwood Timberlands at the foot of the Alabaster Sierras
Uriel cast Pass Without Trace on the party before disembarking (+2 to all stealth checks, 1 hour, no range limit after cast)
Tomis cast Control Weather — "Let the mountain wear its shroud. What moves beneath is of no concern to these sky creatures."

Incrementing precipitation to ground fog over two 1d4 × 10 minute intervals (rolled well — fast transition)
Also incrementing wind to moderate to harry Aarakocra flight
Full 5-mile radius, 8-hour duration

Grimbold summoned a Phantom Steed (warg) for the ascent; Freya rode it given her halved ground speed
Rowan and Grimbold mounted Juniper; Tomis hovering; party otherwise on foot
Session ended at the start of a skill challenge: how quickly and how unnoticed can the party scale the mountain within the Control Weather window
