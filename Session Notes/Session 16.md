# Session 16

Began just after the attack, hurried off to round up the escaped horses, but only managed to save two of the three which escaped. The other was killed by more ravengers. After a lot of arguing about the proper course of action, we continued toward Jorenn Village where we were met on the wall by a gnome who let us in. Then stayed in the large inn, where we were thoroughly watched and spied upon. Then managed to find Joptic's house where we discovered he had been interrupted during his work.

Jorenn Village: rather diverse, 2/3rds human, streets dirty in need of repair, large battlements
