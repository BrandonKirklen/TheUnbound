#### Starting the Invocation 

"Oh, great Matron of Ravens, watcher of the cycles from life to death, lend us your gaze this night. Witness our plea, embrace this lost spirit. From the shadows of the Shadowfell, across the storm's fierce boundary, we call thee. Return to us, this child of the mountain."

Returning soul, the Matron demands of thee and thine a promise. To cherish and defend, to learn and to protect. Will you uphold the sacred balance as guardian of this mountain and also its creatures, particularly these bulettes of the Offending Pass.

Ku'tun, do you promise to guide your son in this duty, teaching him the ways of the mountain, the secrets of the stone, and the lore of the bulettes that roam this rugged land?

By accepting this charge, young guardian, you embrace a role of great importance. May your path be guided by the wisdom of the Matron and the enduring strength of the mountain. Together, may you and your father protect the balance of our land and the creatures that dwell within it.
