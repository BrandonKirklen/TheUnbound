10th of sunsheer

Starting out to the north west.

Harvest Rise party themed Winter's Crest - theme for the festivities, small snowflakes falling during the day, snowmen made around the town, ice skates areas.

Made it to the northern corner in the afternoon, it was fall themed. Giant table of food, full of halflings, mainly family of the Harths. More ravenger attacks from the north areas for the past year and a half.

11th of sunsheer

By noon on our journey north we've left civilization. Very dead area, looks like the shadegrass is being harvested. Some amount of other planar energy. Jorenn Village started as a silver rush. Curse is v old.

Evening of the 11th, ambushed on the plains of the black valley. They lay in wait until the second watch of the evening, distracting us by tripping our alarm with what was supposed to be a ravager scout. Meanwhile, two bugbears climbed up and over the cliff we were camped upon while a deranged sorcerer attacked from the rear.
