## Character Notes - Tomis

Tribe Structure

Distributed leadership, more a band/collective than anything organized. Somewhat ritualistic and spiritual, natural spirits are important. Care takers of the forest.

Individual -> Family (2-5) -> Band (30-50) -> Tribe (Dawnmist Centaurs)

Tomis’s full name is Tomis Tor, but doesn’t use his family name after what happened to his son.

Character Motivations

Processing the roles of various forces in his life. Seeing a once prosperous group scattered by repeated attacks makes it difficult for him to trust forces beyond other than the nature he sees present.

Rewarded for his interest in the Matron with a letter and gifts from her, Tomis is wanting to fulfill her trust in him by seeking out the other items of her power. But how to speak to the group about it...

Timeline

Aarakocra move against the centaurs coming down from the hills and scattering the tribes which once lived peacefully together. A day known to centaurs as piercing the low lying clouds. Tomis’s yearling son, El-li, was taken from the herd that day and hasn’t been seen since. The herd disbanded after this, scattering to friends and relatives around the country to rebuild. Tomis, obsessed with understanding why El-li was taken from him, traveled north to study the gods, finding solace with The Matron of Ravens who taught of the cycle of life and that we all must find an end. Assisting there in the study of those who would attempt to pervert this balance, Tomis created his own path, while his mate Lilic Tor stayed with her friends within the herd.

Many years later, Tomis left Whitestone to be closer to the natural life he grew up with, settling in the Dawnmist Pines with a few tatters of his old family. The band has settled in a large cave, near the forest center, beneath the roots of one of the larger trees, sheltering them as they range and cultivate the wildlife around them. Druids abound and the beasts maintain a close watch on their charges. Semi-annual gatherings at various Occasionally trading with tribes around them, mostly the pelts of the animals they care for. The centaurs strive to have nature exist in balance and have no qualms about sacrificing animal lives to bolster their own, provided the forest can sustain what is taken.

Tomis’s two best friends in the nomadic herds are Nara and Nessus Gri. A mated pair originally from the Dawnmists, they were one of the first to welcome Tomis when he ventured from Whitestone some 10 odd years ago when the pair were still young. Nessus is still affectionately called “the Colt” by Tomis, despite being in his late 20’s now. The three of them will often hunt together, travel the forest as a group, visit other small groups. Nara, a natural talent with all things Druidic, often pokes fun at the more rugged Tomis and Nessus, both preferring to hunt than have anything to do with subtlety.

Other Individuals

Limited metal working, limited to studding their leather. Some rudimentary forging equipment on the southeastern edge near Turst Fields, can be utilized by any centaur who needs it, run by Rynna Zag, who likes to tinker with metal.

### Other Text Blobs 

A fun fact about Tomis, your centaur character, could be that his dual nature as both a powerful, 1500-pound warrior and a life domain cleric gives him a unique connection between the physical and spiritual worlds. As a centaur, Tomis can use his immense strength and speed to protect others, but as a cleric of the Matron of Ravens, he also holds deep knowledge of life and death, healing, and guiding souls to the afterlife. This makes him not only a formidable combatant but also a revered spiritual guide, balancing the raw power of his centaur form with the wisdom and reverence for life and death granted by his deity. His imposing presence may strike fear into his enemies, but his healing touch and spiritual insight bring comfort to his allies.

A fun fact about Tomis’ backstory is that, despite his large and imposing stature, his path to becoming a life domain cleric began with a deeply personal tragedy. In his youth, Tomis was a warrior, fiercely protective of his herd. However, during a raid, he was unable to save his young son, who fell in a raid from the aracokra. This loss haunted him, leading him to seek solace and answers in the divine. His prayers were eventually answered by the Matron of Ravens, who showed him the delicate balance between life and death. She revealed that while death is inevitable, it can also be a sacred transition, not a failure.

This revelation shifted his purpose from one of pure combat to one of healing and guiding others, so no one would have to face the same sorrow unprepared. Now, as a cleric, Tomis draws strength from his connection to the Matron, using his powers to heal, protect, and ensure that the souls of the fallen find their way to the afterlife with dignity and peace.

Another fun fact about Tomis’ backstory is that before dedicating himself fully to the Matron of Ravens, he spent years as a wanderer, feeling lost between his warrior roots and his newfound spiritual calling. During this time, Tomis became known as the “Wandering Hoof,” traveling across distant lands and helping those in need—both through his combat skills and his emerging clerical abilities.

His reputation grew as a mysterious figure who could arrive unexpectedly, heal the wounded, defeat marauding threats, and then disappear into the wild without asking for thanks or recognition. These travels were not just physical journeys but also spiritual ones, as Tomis wrestled with the idea of mortality, not only for others but for himself. It was during one of these wandering years that he received a direct vision from the Matron of Ravens, calling him to embrace his destiny fully. This moment solidified his path, and ever since, Tomis has been a cleric not just of healing, but of deep purpose—honoring life by preparing souls for the next stage of their existence.

Note from Raven

Tomis,
You and those you travel with have stumbled upon happenings of a magnitude you cannot possibly comprehend. This grand entanglement may be my only hope. Events are transpiring that cause my power to wane, both on your plane and my own. As twilight befalls me, I have mustered what little capability I have beyond the gate to bless each of you.

The weave of fate must occasionally be stepped outside of in order for the stability of our worlds to be maintained. Destiny is a yoke that keeps the world sane, but you and those you travel with are no longer bound by it. Use this. It is the last remnant of what I can do on this plane as my power wanes. There will be a time where the world will need you all to be its agents, but until then you must grow together.
Gather your strength. Grow your bonds. Keep them alive, my child.

As you grow together, there are artifacts in this world like the ones you found today. These relics of a bygone era still wield great power, and they will aid you in the trials you have yet to face. Gather them if you can, though many are lost and those that are not are kept jealously by those who have them.
The first, you have already found. They are blades originally crafted by acolytes of The Forgotten One. They were originally used to slay kings, but eventually were used by him in his ascension. I found them and used them to strike him down and restore order to the weave of fate, and as such they called to you all as my chosen.

The second, the Needle of the Moonweaver should appeal to one who you travel with. It is a needle by which she weaves her tapestry on this plane, through her acolytes weaving the blade through her adversaries. It was last seen in the hands of an adventurer whose fate was to be eaten by a green dragon in the Rifenmist Jungle.

Third, the dwarf Tygore Kraghammer, of whom the city is named once had an artifact of great power. It was said to stay his nerves in the direst of situations, like those that you will surely find yourself facing soon. I know not what befell Tygore or where his artifact may lay, as it was before my time, but I know it is real and was bestowed upon him by the Allhammer. He may aid you if he wishes it to be found.

The last two are held by good men. The challenge will be not in finding them, but in convincing them that your cause is just enough for them to part with them. First, the leader of the Temple of the Stormlord in Vasselheim wears a belt of great power. It has kept him alive through a long life.

Lastly, the armor of my previous champion. His friends now carry it, and convincing them to part with the armor of their fallen brother may be the hardest challenge of all.

Divining these was the last of my power on this realm. The next time we speak you will be contacting me. Do not fail me Tomis, for the future is now uncertain. My path of the world splits ahead, and one road will mean not just the end of me but of many on both our realms.

## Tomis's Speach

Good people of Kraghammer, we come before your glorious city having arrived as outsiders, passing by the giggling fields of wildwood gnomes enjoying a rich mushroom stews, sending away our flathooved ponies in exchange for the dexteritous long horned sheep of the carvers, to be received and humbled the far-sighted and far-traveled Wanda Glorenthal. With an unncorruptable heart, she invited us into your city to work towards our shared goal of stability and justice not only for Kraghammer, but for all in our our shared land. We sought naught for ourselves but a warm bed at the Ironhearth tavern, good company and competition (gestures to Bargast), good food (gestures to the table) and some tools for our continued quest.

Through the wise gentle guidance of Blenton Zuurthom as well as generous bit of helping lubrication of wine from Kraghammer’s finest brewery, we learned more about the proud and noble history of this city as well as the aprocraphal sword of damnaclese hanging over the throne of the ironkeaper.

I speak of the prophecy of Trygore had remained unsolved for the last 500 years, with its historical artifacts, his treasure, its glory in unity all remaining locked away behind the steadfast leaders here. Having swayed the Zuurthoms with our straightforward and unabashed commitment to dwarven unity and after having our own justice and morality throughly examined, we had the first of the word of this prophecy.

Next, we survived the two headed Ettin of the Thunderbrands, starting with the bombastic teleportation of Drake Thunderbrand and piercing questioning of Cendra Thunderbrand, then journeying deep into the hall of burning mushrooms only to be stymied by an enemy from below. We stumbled upon the war camp of none other than Grickfeld sent by Zargath himself to align their evil purpose with our present threat of Despit Darren of the Duraguard in an underhanded attack on Kraghammer.

In a long and difficult struggle in the artificial light in the underdark, at the cost of one of our own, we defeated the Warleader, uncovering his plans as well as his tunnels, carved by agony and pain itself driving towards the heart of dwarven labor, ingenuity, and people, the quarry of the bronzegrips. Stopping not but for a moment to grieve for the loss of one so close, we pressed on to find an attack already underway. With the support of the Thunderbrands, our own Urial threw himself into the hell that had become the quarry seeking to find our ally Wanda and her secret critical rescue.

Through grim fighting, whispered coordination in the dark, and more than a godsend of luck, Urial and Wanda rescued none other than the fiery coordinator and stalwart defendant daughter of Nostic the ironkeaper Barbeda.
