# Freya Description

<img src="Freya%20Killing%20Aarakocra.jpeg" alt="Freya swinging the hellfire-lit Cleaver of the Black Crown at an Aarakocra in the Alabaster Sierras" width="400">

**The Cursed Zealot of the Black Crown**

**Race:** Half-Orc

**Class:** Zealot Barbarian

**Age:** Late 20s in appearance (truly ancient)

**Height:** 6’2”

**Build:** Powerfully built, with the physique of someone who’s lived by the blade and died by powerful magic. Sharply defined muscles, every tendon visible beneath skin toughened by centuries of battle.

**Overview:**

Freya appears to be a late 20s, 6’2” half-orc zealot barbarian, cursed to walk the world long after death should have claimed her. She has a well defined face and body, covered in scars from countless battles during an era of warring gods. Despite her youth in appearance, her aura is ancient and relentless — a creature half made of fury, half made of grief.

**Physical Appearance:**

Her orcish green skin is vibrant but scarred — deep claw marks across her ribs, burns on her shoulders, and thin white scars across her forearms and neck.

Freya’s hair is long, wavy, and dark with an iridescent sheen from the magic infusing her — a ghostly play of purple, green, and blue highlights that catch torchlight like oil on water. She ties it back in a loose, high tail.

Across Freya’s back and shoulders lies the ancient sigil of her curse — a web of glowing arcane tattoos burned into her flesh by Vespin Chloras himself during the Age of Arcanum.

**Clothing & Adornments:**

Freya wears practical, battle-worn attire, heavy with blood, grime, and ash. Her chest is wrapped in stained torn cloth bandages. A single leather strap harness crosses her torso, securing her greataxe.

Her fur kilt and leather wraps are equally worn, darkened by dried blood. Her boots are new magic items, the Boots of Cloud Jumping, with adornments of clouds. She also has a cloak of griffin feathers that crackles with lightning. The handle of her axe goes under the cloak, with the head protruding behind her head.

**Weapon — The Cleaver of the Black Crown:**

Freya’s greataxe is massive, double-headed, and unmistakably infernal in origin. Its metal is black with infernal runes covering it completely. The runes pulse with a hellfire red light when activated, and the axe edges themselves will light with that same hellfire.

The axe also has a necrotic energy that draws the life directly from Freya when activated, with her flesh necrotizing the more power she draws from the axe itself.
