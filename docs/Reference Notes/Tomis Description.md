# Tomis Description

| :---: | :---: |

**Race:** Centaur

**Class:** Cleric (Life Domain, Matron of Ravens)

**Age:** 48

**Height:** 16 hands (approx. 5’4” at the withers, 7’2” overall when upright)

**Weight:** \~1500 lbs

**Build:** Towering, muscular centaur physique, hunts and does physical labor in the dawnmist pines imposing, yet practiced graceful

**Face & Hair:**

Tomis has a grisled, strong-boned face with a calm but intense expression, thoughtful eyes and a vigilant demeanor. His hair is chestnut brown with a few prominent white streaks running through both his beard and mane. Hair is medium-long and often braided like his main and tail. His flank has a white handprint from the Matron of Ravens.

**Expression & Mood:**

Serious and determined, not grim. 

**Armor & Equipment:**

* **Full Plate Armor:** Polished but worn from battle, with crudely but carefully etched patterns suggesting ancient oaths and divine symbolism. Armor covers both his humanoid torso and equine flanks.

* **Shadow Ward Cloak:** An ethereal, black cloak that ripples with barely contained darkness, as if absorbing the moonlight. It trails and lifts unnaturally, resembling shifting smoke.

* **Stormkeeper’s Belt:** A wide, storm-forged belt with crackling lightning motifs etched in silver and bronze. A large circular clasp sits at its center, sometimes faintly glowing with static arcs.

* **Spellguard Tower Shield:** Large, steel-toned with a matte finish, bearing the emblem of the **Matron of Ravens** a stylized black raven with wings flared in an angular, divine shape.

* **Mace:** A heavy, consecrated weapon with glowing runes and a glowing gemstone core.
