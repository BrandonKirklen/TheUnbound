Opening Statement

- Lady Cassandra 
- Timothy Locke
- Kaley Shorthall
- Jubilee Shortall
- Jared Howard

My Lady Cassandra, and Council, thank you for receiving me on such short notice. I bring an account of our recent actions at the ruined castle, the fate of those who stood between Tal’Dorei and a greater catastrophe, and the current state of the divine struggle now made visible to our people.

To begin, I will speak plainly. The mission was successful. The cost was severe and still mounting.

---

Objective and Initial Findings

Our primary objective was to disrupt the ongoing apotheosis of the entity known as the Forgotten One, whose influence you have already felt through the mass vision forced upon the populace.

---

Approach to the Basin and Enemy Disposition

Our forces entered into a vast basin-shaped depression, flat at the bottom and enclosed by sheer stone walls on all sides. Snow had collected unevenly along the floor, marked by extensive troop movement, indicating long-standing occupation and readiness.

Key observations upon approach:
	-	Prepared defensive posture, clearly anticipating a direct assault.
	-	Thirty-foot towers ringed the basin at regular intervals.
	-	A massive bipedal Adranach stood at the center, with numerous smaller constructs positioned throughout.
	-	An estimated one thousand troops posted within the basin.
  - Many airships
	-	Numerous overlapping arcane and physical defensive layers were visible even from altitude.

At the heart of the basin, we observed Keyleth engaged in spellcasting, preparing a wide-area of weather of catastrophic scale.

---

Opening of the Battle – The Tempest Unleashed

The engagement began with overwhelming elemental force.
	-	A massive acid spell, spanning nearly seven hundred and twenty feet, tore through the basin.
	-	Lightning, hail, and wind followed in rapid succession.
	-	The battlefield became a storm made manifest – a true Tempest, shaped and sustained by druidic will.

Simultaneously, forces began to crest the basin’s rim.

---

Allied Ground Forces and Multi-Front Assault

From the southern lip, the Forces of Terra advanced:
	-	Stoneguard formations led the descent.
	-	Fire Ashari followed, coating advancing troops in ash and flame.
	-	Entire units slid down the basin walls as burning meteors, striking enemy lines with devastating effect.

Additional allied actions included:
	-	Kraghammer goats spreading along the rim, with dwarven rifle teams providing suppressive fire from above.
	-	A coordinated weather control effort, culminating in the manifestation of a massive stone giant shaping atmospheric conditions over the basin.
	-	Patice, the Heart of the Mountain, opened a subterranean passage, allowing troops to surge directly into the basin interior.

Enemy responses were immediate and brutal:
	-	The central Adranach emitted a focused plasma beam, breaching sections of the basin wall.
	-	Hound-headed entities mobilized along the perimeter.
	-	Enemy wizards mounted on winged constructs rose from below to intercept our airships.

--- 

Air Engagement and Advance on the Castle

The battle escalated into the air.
	-	Phyrrus distinguished himself by eliminating multiple enemy wizards as they rose to meet us.
	-	We cleared the airship base under heavy fire and advanced toward the castle proper.
	-	Our vessel endured sustained bombardment from mobile flak constructs, stone boxes that rocked the ship with repeated impacts.

During this phase:
	-	Fenric revealed his true nature, fading into an extraplanar construct armed with four vibrating blades.
	-	He identified himself as an Inevitable, a Coiluratte, among the least of their order but purpose-built for investigation and enforcement.
	-	He descended, hovering above the battlefield as the assault continued.

Behind us, the scale of the battle became fully apparent:
	-	Massive meteors struck portions of the enemy’s frugal-class forces.
	-	Alura raised protections around the largest allied ship, preventing catastrophic loss.

---

Upon breaching the upper levels of the castle, we found a critical surprising truth:
	-	The prior Wondermaker of the League of Miracles, Escal Renyndan, had not escaped nor perished.
	-	He was captured and broken, held in place to maintain a mental and arcane linkage between the spellwrites that fueled the ritual.
	-	We believe this linkage was essential to sustaining the apotheosis in progress.

It is now understood that the Wondermaker himself had been transformed, willingly or otherwise, into a pawn of the Nagpa, bound in service to the Forgotten One.

---

Enemy Forces and Escalation

Within the observatory tower we encountered:
  - A long time rival, one of the newest spellwrights of presumed critical import to the continued success
	-	Three Nagpa, each bearing distinct armaments and arcane markings.
	-	One, physically larger and bearing runes similar to Escal’s
	-	The Nagpa were acting in coordination, with clear command hierarchy and explicit objectives, including:
	-	Locating and destroying the Matron of Ravens.
	-	Recovering or restoring the Forgotten One’s lost true name.
	-	Mobilizing Aarakocra forces and sowing chaos among Tal’Dorei’s defenders.

During this confrontation, the ritual advanced. Vas Machina was actively engaged with them. We saw Scandlin wish back into existance Vax, Champion of the Matron of Ravens. 

This was clearly an attempt to weaken the Matron as she poured large amounts of her remaining power into bringing him here and exposed herself to their trap.

A shadowed entity manifested, speaking in the voice of the Forgotten One himself.

Sacrifice of the Deliverance

At the moment the apotheosis threatened to complete, the crew of the Deliverance acted.

Know this clearly, my Lady:
	-	They understood what they were doing.
	-	They chose it.

In a final, coordinated assault, the crew gave their lives to disrupt the ritual lattice and sever the Wondermaker’s sustaining link. Their actions:
	-	Interrupted the apotheosis at its final stage.
	-	Prevented the Forgotten One from fully manifesting as a god of death.
	-	Blocked his attempt to reclaim or restore his true name, which appears to be central to his claim on divine authority.

Without their sacrifice, I would not be standing before you today. 

---

Retreat and Aftermath

With the ritual destabilized and enemy forces converging, we sounded an immediate retreat and escaped through planar means, ultimately returning to Vasselheim.

There, we took further action:
	-	We successfully restored additional strength to the Matron of Ravens, who remains:
	-	Hidden.
	-	Active.
	-	Wounded, but no longer fading.
	-	She is aware of the struggle, aware of the cost, and continues to act within the narrow constraints left to her.

The Forgotten One’s attempt to redirect the “spigot of belief” away from her failed, due in no small part to the interruption at the castle and subsequent intervention.

---

Strategic Implications

Based on testimony from the High Archivist of Vasselheim and our own experiences, we believe the following to be true:
	-	Names hold power, the details of which we're still unsure, possibly more than domains or portfolios.
	-	The Forgotten One is attempting to:
	  -	Exploit our fear and doubt. 
	  -	Bind divine authority through intersections of divinity, naming, and divination.
	  -	The ritual we disrupted mirrors one originally stolen from the Matron of Ravens, likely tied to leyline manipulation and belief cultivation.

This conflict is not over. It has merely been slowed.

---

Recommended Next Steps

In light of these findings, I offer the following recommendation to Whitestone and Tal’Dorei at large:
	-	We must bolster public belief and reverence for the Matron of Ravens, openly and deliberately.
	-	Silence and secrecy have kept her alive, but belief sustains her.
	-	Memorials, rites, and spoken names matter now more than ever.

The people have already seen the vision. Fear has spread. That fear will feed something, my Lady. We should ensure it does not feed him.

---

Closing

The crew of the Deliverance died so that the world might continue without a false god of death standing over it.

Their names deserve to be spoken.
Their choice deserves to be known.
And the Matron they served still stands, even now.

I place this account, and my continued service, at Whitestone’s disposal.
