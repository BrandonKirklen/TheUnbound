#### Tomis' Words of Wisdom
> "Back in the Dawnmist Pines, we had a trader who'd make promises sweeter than honey-wine. He'd scribble out contracts in beautiful ink, with seals and ribbons and all the flair of a royal decree. But here's the thing—every year, come winter, he'd vanish without a trace. Never fulfilled a single clause. Folks tried to hold him to it, but what's parchment to a man with no fixed address and no sense of shame?  
>
> Lesson stuck with me: a contract, no matter how sacred or arcane, is only as strong as the one who can enforce it. Divine or fey, it still comes down to who shows up when it breaks."
