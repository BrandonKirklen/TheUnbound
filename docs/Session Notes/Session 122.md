### Recap

The session opened with a table-wide recap of [Session 121](Session%20121.md): the party's arrival and shopping trip in Grimbold's homeland of Niirdal-Poc, the discovery of the **Orb of Avalir** (once belonging to the Archmage **Paysha Porco**) at the Cobalt Soul auction house, the tense debate over the **Crown of the Mindmaster Matriarch** that ended with Grimbold attuning to it on a two-day trial, and Tomis's Commune with the Matron of Ravens about the Nagpa's true objective in Vasselheim.

From there the session picked up mid-shopping-trip at **Basque Tellin's** supply shop in Niirdal-Teek, before following the party through the rest of Grimbold's homecoming, the trip back to Vasselheim, and a chaotic final hour that ended the session on a cliffhanger.

### Session Notes

#### Finishing Up in Niirdal-Teek

- Grimbold checked with Basque Tellin on rarer spells: **Planar Binding** required an availability roll of 18+ and came up short (rolled a 3); no one on hand could cast Water Breathing either. Basque offered to spend a couple of days quietly checking with the wider faculty for anything higher-level, since the party was leaving too soon to wait on a rush answer.
- Purchased scrolls: **Invisibility, Earthbind, 2× Vortex Warp, 1× Knock ("Nox"), 2× Pyrotechnics,** and a **3rd-level Remove Curse** — entrusted to Tomis rather than Rowan, after Rowan begged off ("I go down in the fight first, historically"). Roughly 900 gp spent on 2nd-level scrolls, ~2,500 gp left of the party's shopping cash after ~8,000 gp spent this trip.
- Explored (but tabled) commissioning a scroll of **Circle of Power** for Rowan — no faculty paladin on hand powerful enough, and no time to wait for one to be found and paid for properly. Left as a "next visit" possibility.

#### Grimbold and Grimcold Compare Notes

- Grimbold and his simulacrum **Grimcold** linked telepathically (forehead to the crown's ruby gem) to share what each had learned. Grimcold relayed his read on the **Orb of Avalir**: it isn't just Paysha Porco's private journal but a deliberate archive of **Age of Arcanum knowledge** — likely the very reason the Cobalt Soul was founded, to police exactly this kind of dangerous, valuable knowledge. Heavy overlap with the enchantment-focused notes taken off the slain Nagpa means studying the two together could meaningfully accelerate any future high-level spell research.

> Grimbold: "We probably have to read about the calamity part."

- **Major lore reveal:** Paysha Porco's grandfather, **Emir Porco**, was the wizard who invented **Brumestone** — the substance that lets airships and flying cities fly. Brumestone isn't understood or replicable in the current era; what exists is scavenged, not made, and extremely valuable for its scarcity. This immediately caught Grimcold's attention given the party's ongoing entanglement with flying-city technology.
- **Freya strongly objected** to Grimbold pursuing a deeper look at the Orb: it holds the actual knowledge that triggered the Calamity, and belonged to Paysha Porco and the **Brass Ring** faction blamed for it — "if you wanted to do it again, there's a bit of a blueprint there." Rowan needled Grimbold that as a sworn Cobalt Soul member he arguably has an obligation to lock it away rather than study it; Grimbold deflected and tabled the idea for after Vasselheim.

#### Planning Session: What Does the Enemy Actually Want?

The party compiled what they know of the enemy's objectives:

- Confirmed or suspected: the true **name of their god**, the **Matron of Ravens'** location (both likely reachable only through the Feywild), and the **Tome of Banishment**.
- The trammel-thirds the party already surrendered to the Whitestone vault weren't the point — hostile forces were already headed toward Whitestone regardless, so nothing was lost by giving them up.
- **New DM reveal:** the enemy needs **one more thing**, beyond the trammels and the Tome, to complete whatever they're planning — left unspecified. *(Open thread.)*
- Since the god/Matron leads point toward the Feywild rather than Vasselheim, the party concluded **Vasselheim is the far more likely real target** of the coming attack.
- **Rowan's personal stake:** **Lady Kima** and **Alurah's** wife are trapped in Emon since it began flying. Rowan attended their wedding and calls Kima "one of the most powerful paladins I've ever met" — he has a personal interest in helping liberate the city and check on her.
- **Freya's plan, adopted by the party:** rather than out-think the enemy, use the attack itself as cover — infiltrate one of the two flying cities while it's busy assaulting Vasselheim, search for survivors and intel on who or what controls it (the last flying city they fought was dragon-piloted), and try to remove "one big bad from the board" — ideally another Nagpa, or a controlling dragon.
- **Agreed plan:** get to Vasselheim, link up with the defenders to learn their plan, then pick a flying city to hit.

#### The Homecoming Party

- At dusk, Grimbold led the party to Niirdal-Poc's communal gathering bowl — an earthen amphitheater with fire pits and a cleared dance area, the community's holiday space. ~50–60 attendees, mostly beastfolk, only one or two humans; culture shock ran both directions between the party and the isolated community. No meat anywhere (Niirdal-Poc keeps no livestock), prompting a running joke about Grimbold's canonical love of fowl ("Not a chicken to be seen!").
- Grimbold was swarmed as the hometown hero; the party split up to mingle. **Tomis**, unusually comfortable in this kind of tight-knit gathering (it echoes his own tribal upbringing), actively worked the crowd rather than trailing along. **Rowan** enjoyed the small-town scale and tried unfamiliar fruit.
- Grimbold introduced **Grimcold** to old friends as "a magical creation... a clone of me" — novel magic even here.
- **Dugan** (an old dwarf friend, red-bearded, nearly Grimbold's height) caught him up: in his absence, the community handed the "Chief of Magical Affairs" post to Grimbold's old wizard-school rival, **Raybor** — Grimbold's instant, visible dislike ("You know I hate Raybor... he's probably been embezzling") reads as equal parts old rivalry and real jealousy.
- **Small-world moment:** Dugan's crew recently guided a visiting adventuring party (two half-elves, an older woman, a younger human man) to a dragon hoard containing the famous rapier **Vestige of Divergence** — the very party revealed to be connected to **Vex'ahlia of Vox Machina**, the same figure the Unbound themselves recently raided a vault tied to for their own dragon-hoard loot. Grimbold recognized one name from the description but the audio doesn't preserve it clearly.

#### The Farrah/Leona Mystery

The session's most unsettling thread. Overhearing Dugan and Grimbold's catch-up, **Uriel** picked up on two names — **Aei'Tu** and his sister **Leona** — and the table called for a **DC 30 Charisma save** to push past what turned out to be an unusually resistible suppression effect (framed as overcomable only because of the party's exceptional saves). Rowan couldn't roll at all — in his personal history he never met Leona. **Tomis passed** (with advantage from the Spellguard Shield) and immediately felt something was deeply wrong.

- Tomis pulled Rowan aside: **Leona** — formerly known as "Stinky," later as **Pharra** — was a party-adjacent traveler Tomis once tried to **Revivify, and it failed**. She is confirmed dead in their own history. Yet Dugan just described a living "Leona," sister of Aei'Tu, traveling with the party that found the Vestige of Divergence.
- **Tomis's Commune with the Matron** (mid-party, d100 roll of 52 — successful):
  1. Was there something strange/wrong with Leona? → **Yes.**
  2. Were the party's memories tampered with? → **No.**
  3. Is Leona dead? → **Yes.**
- Rowan was openly skeptical this is external manipulation at all — floating that Tomis may be having a mental break rather than seeing a genuine impostor, though he concedes archfey are canonically capable of "funny things with names." He advised setting it aside for the party and dealing with it later; privately (per his player) he wrote it off as "95% Thomas has broken his mind."
- **Left unresolved.** A running thread for a future, more targeted Commune about whoever is "masquerading as Leona."

#### Grandma Opie and the Vanished Mother

- Grimbold introduced Tomis to **Grandma Opie**, hoping Tomis could scry her going forward — she'd already wandered off by the time they found her again ("she's so goddamn spry," "she ditched us again").
- Grimbold's backstory, filled in for the table: raised in and out of the **Sands of Sanctuary** orphanage whenever his mother **Jody** couldn't care for him — not her fault, "she had you, not you have her." First cast Mage Hand at age 6, inspired by seeing **Scanlan Shorthalt** fly into town on Bigby's Hand and imagining his unknown father was a great sorcerer. Grandma Opie and Jody were both encouraging at the time.
- Uriel's read on the evening: aside from Dugan and Drek, none of Grimbold's old classmates engage with him closely — polite-acquaintance energy only. The orphanage sounds more "cushy resort" than traumatic, but there's a clear absence of the tight community warmth Uriel recognizes from his own Ashari upbringing. Grimbold was likely a bit insufferable and self-isolating as a kid, and has visibly mellowed since.
- **New complication, as the party said goodnight:** Dugan revealed — assuming Grimbold already knew — that Jody **left Niirdal-Poc a couple of weeks ago** with a man named **Sethu**, apparently to go looking for Grimbold. Grandma Opie never mentioned it either. Grimbold, blindsided and guilty about his own lack of contact, declined help searching but asked to be kept posted. *(Open thread.)*
- Closing insight check (rolled a 5): the old friendships, while still warm, have visibly grown apart — neither side is quite who the other remembers.

#### Return to Vasselheim

- Before departing, Rowan shared a rare sincere moment: his long-lived half-elf children and grandchildren have all died of old age; only great-great-grandchildren remain, and his deepest personal stake in the whole campaign is wanting to eventually rejoin his family in death through the Matron's grace.
- The party skipped the **Hexahedron of Sarqet** side-trip (time pressure) and teleported from Niirdal-Poc's farmland to the base of Vasselheim's **1,000 Steps** (fresh d100 roll of 56, on target, since Grimbold's only object tied to Vasselheim — a Cobalt Soul door fragment — was ruled too indirect a link).
- **Notable reveal on arrival:** only **half of the Titan** remains visible overhead — the Dawn Marshals' planned strike against it went through, but this has created a new, unresolved problem: the **Matron of Ravens' material-plane hiding place was inside the Titan**, and with half of it gone, that concealment may now be compromised. *(Open thread.)*
- Lore along the climb: Grimbold left Rexentrum's Archive for Vasselheim over "office politics." Rexentrum is the capital of the **Dwendalian Empire** and seat of the **Cerberus Assembly** — the world's most powerful (and most closely watched) arcane authority, with sanctioned/restricted deity worship, an almost total cultural inverse of Vasselheim's embrace of divine worship and suspicion of unchecked arcane magic. Past Cerberus Assembly members include **Delilah Briarwood** (before she and her vampire-convert husband took Whitestone, slaughtering the de Rolos) and **Trent Ikithon**.
- The 1,000 Steps took ~2 hours to climb (Freya rolled a natural 1 on the Constitution save and struggled badly). Vasselheim, on arrival, was more heavily fortified and mobilized than the party has ever seen it — skyward-facing defenses the length of the stairs, a marked increase in wyvern and griffin air patrols, and a skyport crowded with foreign airships from Ank'Harel, Tal'Dorei, and the Dwendalian Empire. Vasselheim prides itself as the only city to have stood unbroken through the Calamity.

#### The Street Preacher

- A lone, friendless man on a Quadroads corner argued publicly that the Forgotten One deserves worship too ("Who are we to judge who deserves a seat at the table? A god denied is a god nonetheless").
- Tomis engaged him in a genuine theological argument — agency of belief, the Matron's own origin as once being "a new god of death" herself, Grimbold recalling the Forgotten One personally "ripping my friend Grog in half." Tomis briefly weighed invoking Divine Intervention to have the Matron simply kill the man; Grimbold half-seriously floated banishing him from the city with the Crown. Rowan and Uriel talked both down — the man has no real audience, and silencing him would only hand him the martyrdom and attention he's after, and would concede that belief itself is something to fear. The party settled for jeering and flipping him off as they walked away.

> Preacher: "Palatable or not, who are we to deny what is true? You need not like the news, but simply the rules of this city must administer him the due respect that is becoming of a god of the prime pantheon."

#### Battle Preparations

- Tomis sent a **Sending** to **Lieve'tel**: the party is in Vasselheim, ready to help, floating a direct assault on Emon. Her reply confirmed the flying cities are expected around **noon, roughly 24 hours out**, and left the party to its own devices until she can deploy them.
- **Lodging debate:** rather than split between the Ravens Crest and the Gilded Griffin, Grimbold proposed an elaborate prep plan — open a **Demiplane** (~2,700 sq ft, bare cobblestone-and-wood) and spend most of the remaining time inside casting multiple **Glyphs of Warding**, pre-loading buffs (**Haste, Fly, Invisibility**) that party members could trigger with a command word mid-battle. The group ultimately decided Tomis and Grimbold alone would go in (Tomis carrying Plane Shift as a safe way back out, capped at 8 hours), while Rowan, Uriel, and Freya went to the **Platinum Sanctuary** to gather intel from the Dawn Marshals on enemy numbers, timing, and how the party factors into the defense.

#### The Incident

The party subtly cast Demiplane at the threshold of Grimbold's chambers in the **Raven's Crest Temple** — casting an **8th-level arcane spell inside a temple of a prime deity, during a citywide war footing**, was detected almost immediately.

- Two **Adjudicators** — Vasselheim's homemade answer to Inevitables, black-armored, featureless bronze-masked constructs built by the high clerics/Dawn Marshals specifically to police this kind of infraction — forced the demiplane door open within minutes of Tomis and Grimbold starting to cast.
- Grimbold tried to bluff his way out ("this is a totally approved business... approved by Thomas") and rolled a **natural 1** on Deception (already at disadvantage). One Adjudicator seized him bodily; Rowan arrived and tried to smooth things over, apologizing and vouching for the party's ties to Lieve'tel, at a Persuasion check set at disadvantage (DC 25 just to avoid a fight, DC 30 to actually preserve relations).
- Grimbold let himself be carried out without resistance — until, ~25 feet from the door, he tried a subtle **Misty Step** back into the demiplane. **Both Adjudicators had Mage Slayer** and got reactions, landing all three power-strike attacks (37, then 86 more). His warding broke and a failed Constitution save (rolled 19 — still short) left him **paralyzed**, one turn from being beaten unconscious.
- With no one else willing to escalate into open combat against the city's own defenders, **Grimcold acted** — grabbed Grimbold and cast **Teleport**, successfully pulling them both out to the same farmland outside Vasselheim's walls. Grimbold and Grimcold vanished from the confrontation.

**The session ended here.**
