# Session 117

**Date:** 13th of Fessuran
**Days Remaining Until Freya (7th of Quen'pillar):** 23

### Recap

The party arrived at the foot of the Alabaster Sierras under cover of Tomis's **Control Weather** (ground fog and rising winds) and Uriel's **Pass Without Trace**, riding Juniper, a Phantom Steed warg, and Tomas's hover. The plan was to scale the mountain unnoticed while the storm grounded the Aarakocra. Somewhere along the climb the party was discovered, engaged, and pushed up to the summit observatory — the source of the mysterious glinting structure Rowan and Uriel had spotted from the river. Combat was already well underway when the session opened, with the Nagpa, his Aarakocra warlord and archer escort, and a Dao on the field.

### Session Notes

**Opening State of the Field**

The session began mid-combat at the observatory, picking up after a partial retcon of the previous round (Tomis had a charge of his Shadow Cloak left that hadn't been used; Kevin walked the damage back, restored Tomas to 43 HP, and let him reaction-halve a great-sword hit and teleport up to a ledge above the door).

The board:

- **Freya**: gone. A pile of dust on the ground after a disintegration beam from one of the True Nightmare illusions. Her gear scattered around the ash.
- **Rowan**: down at 0 HP, paralyzed, locked in some illusory torment courtesy of the Nagpa's True Nightmare.
- **Juniper**: gone.
- **Tomis**: badly wounded but alive, holding the high ledge above the entrance.
- **Grimbold**: up on the observatory roof, mostly unhurt, his Bigby's Hand having released a temporarily-warded Aarakocra warlord ("Primrose") who had since blinked free with a Far Step.
- **Uriel**: up and at 114 HP, still hasted, fully engaged.
- **The Nagpa**: maintaining True Nightmare as five shadowy clones, badly battered with ichor running off all five forms, the real one cycling its position between turns.
- **Three Aarakocra archers** still on the field; **a Dao** flickering harmlessly around Uriel; the **Aarakocra warlord** with his Gambler's Blade nearby.
- The **storm** Tomis had set in motion was still building — heavy rain and high winds, difficult terrain, no flight, blood and ichor washing down the mountainside.

**A New House Rule: Desperate Measures**

Before continuing, Kevin introduced a new mechanic — **Desperate Measures**, lifted shamelessly from Critical Role. While bloodied (below half HP), a character can permanently fail death saving throws (until next long rest) in exchange for various benefits:

- 1 fail → bonus dash + disengage, +5 to a just-failed d20, or reroll an attack
- 2 fails → maximize an attack's damage
- 3 fails (instant death if downed) → extra action / extra leveled spell

While downed, a character with 0 failed saves may either stabilize themselves *or* grant inspiration to an ally within sight. Once you take a Desperate Measure, you can no longer self-stabilize. Inspiration granted this way doesn't carry past the long rest.

**Rowan's Turn — Inspiration and a Plus Five**

Rowan, downed and paralyzed, granted **inspiration to Grimbold**. Then, on his paralysis save, he committed to a Desperate Measure: marked one death save and added +5 to the roll, narratively shaking off the paralysis through sheer proximity to death. Still down, but no longer locked.

**The Nagpa Drops True Nightmare**

After deliberating out loud, Kevin had the Nagpa drop concentration on the 10th-level True Nightmare. His reasoning, told to the players: the spell had eliminated Freya and was destroying Rowan and Tomis, but Grimbold and Uriel — the two threats actually closing on him — had shrugged it off. He pivoted.

He then attempted to bonus-action charm Grimbold again. Grimbold passed (advantage from inspiration, 25), now immune to that effect for 24 hours.

The Nagpa's main action: an 8th-level spell on Uriel. Grimbold counterspelled at 5th level with a successful roll-off (33 vs. 19, with inspiration). The Nagpa was already concentrating on his counterspell, so the party clocked that his reaction was burned for the round — a meaningful opening.

The save came down anyway. Uriel — visions of being flung through a wormhole, cackling voices in unfamiliar planes, his connection to reality severing — failed badly. He could no longer take reactions, with worse to follow.

**Tomis Stabilizes Rowan**

Tomis leapt down from his ledge (athletics with hoof-advantage to stick the landing on the slick stone) and used his **second Channel Divinity** to dump 75 healing on Rowan and himself — 59 to Rowan to bring him back up, 16 to Tomis.

**Grimbold Gets the Grapple**

Grimbold cast **Magic Missile at 6th level** on the Nagpa (28 damage — confirmed the Nagpa had no Shield prepared, no reactions left for the round). He then sent his still-active **Bigby's Hand** in to grapple. The contested check came back 20 on the hand, beating the Nagpa cleanly. Real one, grappled, speed 0.

**Uriel Gets Forcecaged Off The Cliff**

The Nagpa's spell on Uriel resolved on his turn — 84 force damage and stunned, which broke his Haste concentration and dropped him prone. He used a Desperate Measure (+5) to retry the wisdom save and successfully ended the stun on his next turn, but lost the round.

**Round of Stabs**

Three Aarakocra archers advanced and switched to swords. Tomis took 15. Grimbold weathered a hit. The warlord — under the grappling hand — couldn't break free (Grimbold's hand rolled brutally well, including beating the warlord's Indomitable retry).

**The Nagpa Cuts a Backline Deal**

Out of Grimbold's line of sight, the Nagpa cast **Dominate Person** on Grimbold from a clone position deliberately picked to dodge counterspell. Grimbold failed the Wisdom save. The Nagpa then pulled back deeper into the observatory.

Mentally, over the Telepathic Bond, Grimbold's voice came through cheerfully:

> "Hey, I haven't even taken any damage. I countered his spell again!"

And, after Scorching Ray (8 rays at 7th level, half hitting through Tomis's Shield) lit Tomis up for 29 fire damage:

> "Oopsie, my bad. Got things back under control now. You know me — clumsy old Grimbie. Always accidentally launching 8 attacks at my friends."

The party clocked something was wrong, but couldn't see Grimbold to confirm.

**Tomis Sunders the Nagpa's Concentration**

Once Tomis got line of sight, he ran in, cast **Spiritual Sundering**: 26 damage, -5 to mental saves for one minute, *and* a DC 13 Concentration check on the Nagpa. The Nagpa held — barely.

**Bigby's Forceful Hand**

Daniel had been waiting for this. With the hand still on the field, Grimbold (under domination) used its Forceful Hand option to **shove Rowan**. Rowan failed the contested athletics check and went tumbling off the cliff. He cast **Feather Fall** to negate damage, but the hand stayed locked on him as part of the push, dragging him further down. The Nagpa then dropped concentration on Bigby's Hand entirely — "we don't need that anymore where we're going."

The party realized later: the Nagpa's plan was to force Grimbold to teleport with him out of the encounter using **Word of Recall** or similar — the upcast Dominate Person had been bought 8 hours of duration specifically to make that work.

**The Nagpa's Big Spell**

The Nagpa attempted **Prismatic Spray** through all five clones (impossibly visual — five shadowy figures spitting a rainbow cone). Grimbold subtle-spelled a 3rd-level Counterspell at it, but the roll-off missed (2 vs. high). The Nagpa rolled the d8 effect die — landed on poison instead of disintegrate, sparing the party a worse outcome. Grimbold and the warlord both took 27 damage, Grimbold halving with a successful Dex save. Same effect rolled twice meant acid the second time — a mid-tier outcome from a 7th-level spell.

**Rowan Returns to the Fight**

Rowan teleported up to the ledge with a single charge of Misty Step left, then used a Desperate Measure (one death save) to dash + disengage and close. He cast **Banishment on Tomis** — Tomis voluntarily failed the save — pulling Tomis out of the plane to deny the Nagpa his finishing target. Then Rowan blinked again to maintain line of sight.

**Uriel Slays the Nagpa**

After standing from prone, Uriel marked the Nagpa as his quarry and used his Hunter's blindsense on a 30 Perception to identify the real one through the clones. Two attacks landed (29 to-hit, 19 to-hit). On the third attack he used a **Minor Firearm**: 6d10 damage on a constitution save, half on success. He took **two Desperate Measures (2 death saves) to maximize the firearm damage**. The Nagpa passed the con save, took 35 force damage, was not stunned. Uriel then misty-stepped to the roof.

The Nagpa, down to single digits, dropped before he could cast **Circle of Death** — which would have outright killed Uriel. Rowan ran in, magicked himself in front of the Nagpa, and after a missed first swing took a bonus-action attack:

> "for crimes against the arcane and the world — die!"

A **Blinding Smite** cracked into the bird wizard, blade sparking with divine energy. The Nagpa passed the save through Indomitable but still took the damage. With a follow-up **Divine Smite**, Rowan severed the Nagpa's head.

The four illusory clones poofed out of existence in the same instant.

**Uriel Dies**

The fight wasn't over. The Aarakocra warlord — wielding his **Gambler's Blade** (declared hits double damage or deal nothing) — was still up, and he was furious. He came at Uriel.

Uriel set up his quarry mark and another Minor Firearm shot. The warlord passed both saves; Uriel's attack missed. The warlord **riposted** as a reaction. The blade ignored Uriel's resistance with the doubling-down rule. **40 damage to a 30-HP Uriel.**

Uriel went straight to dead. The Raven Queen had previously told Uriel that if he died again, she could not bring him back.

> Tomis: "Why does he never protect himself?"

**Rowan Closes**

The warlord then turned on Rowan — and rolled three doubled-down attacks in a row that all hit. The Gambler's Blade triggered three times, but Kevin's damage rolls were genuinely bad (a string of low rolls on what could have been catastrophic). Rowan survived through the wall of the storm and dropped into smite range. He took two strikes — a Blinding Smite (the warlord passed via Indomitable, took the damage), then a Divine Smite — which ended him.

**Cleanup**

One archer fell to a guiding bolt from Tomis. The remaining archer took a Ray of Frost from Grimbold (movement reduced) and was eventually finished off. The session ended with the party in the observatory:

- **Uriel: dead.** Tomis cast **Gentle Repose** on his body, buying time for a future revivify attempt.
- **Freya: dead.** Tomis scooped up as much of her ashes as he could and gathered her gear.

> Tomis: "I'll go scoop up as much as I can of Freya's ashes and grab her things."

> Rowan: "We got to get out of here. What should we do about Uriel?"
> Tomis: "Take his body with us, of course."

The party began looting the field. The Nagpa carried the standard expected items. The warlord carried — in addition to the Gambler's Blade — a **scroll**. Freya's death cookie has reportedly turned into a **Scroll of Revivify with diamond cost pre-paid** on her remains.

The party discussed exfil. Tomis's **Word of Recall** is anchored to Vasselheim, which is wildly inconvenient. Grimbold has four 1st-level slots and limited high-level spells remaining. The session ended with the party still in the observatory, considering options.
