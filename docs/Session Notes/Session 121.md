### Session Notes

#### Arrival in Niirdal-Poc — The Tetrarchy Gate

- Two bronze-skinned guards — one half-elf, one hobgoblin, bearing the sigil of the **Tetrarchy** — challenged the party in **Qoniiran**. Grimbold answered in kind and asked the way to the auction house.
- **Tomis cast Tongues on himself** to follow the conversation. The guards visibly relaxed once they recognized Grimbold wasn't just speaking Qoniiran via magic, but as a **native speaker**.

> Guard, clasping a fist to his chest: "Welcome home. Keep your accomplices in line — you are responsible for their whereabouts while they are here."

- Grimbold gave his name in full: **Grimbold Hagellagel** — and told them he'd be staying in a suburb on the western edge of the university district.
- **Uriel and Rowan (with Grimbold's help), plus Freya:** Arcana/history checks on entry. Niirdal-Poc rang only faint bells for Rowan — old, barely mapped, mostly known secondhand as territory the **Iron Authority** claims but has never actually conquered.

#### Touring Grimbold's Homeland

- Grimbold guided the party through the city center, comparing it favorably to Emon in scale but grander — deep crimson adobe shaped by magic rather than hewn, wide boulevards built for a much larger population than currently lives there (roughly **10% occupied**), self-sustaining permanent magic keeping lights, temperature, and even food production running without visible labor.
- **History:** Niirdal-Poc was originally **five distinct cities**, each with its own elected Tetrarch, that have since folded into one under a faded federal structure. The Tetrarchs **disappeared** at the start of the Calamity — no mass graves, no confirmed fate. Grimbold's own research in Rexentrum and Vasselheim never turned up an answer; folk theory holds some of the city's many druids simply "became trees" and are still out there.
- **Tomis**, watching the tight-knit, low-guard-presence neighborhoods, felt an unexpected wistfulness — the small-cluster warmth reminded him of the herd, a contrast to the impersonal bustle of Vasselheim and Whitestone.
- **Freya** — one of the few survivors to escape the flying city of **Avalir** before it was lost in the Calamity, having been experimented on there by **Vespin Chloras** — found Niirdal-Poc a smaller, grounded echo of her lost home: familiar comforts, but without the freedom regular people had back then.

#### The Auction House — Registration and the Frozen Lightning Bolt

- The party located the auction house — **Felice in Yonah Park**. The registrar, an elephantine man named **Selvic**, explained the house's model: high-value items go to bid rather than fixed price, and anything deemed unfit to circulate is simply bought and kept by the institution itself, with compensation to the original owner.
- The next public auction is **3 days out**, drawing largely from a recent **Eiselcross expedition** turning up Age of Arcanum relics — too long a wait given the party's timeline, so they arranged an early appraisal instead.
- **Rowan** recognized a displayed relic — a literal frozen bolt of lightning — as tied to the Champion of Pelor's fight with the **Rime Lord** in northern Tal'Dorei centuries ago, and named the Champion himself from personal history: **Sunbringer**.
- The party registered under **"the Unbound"** rather than a personal name, deliberately, after what happened with Pyrrhus.

#### The Orb of Avalir

- Grimbold's Cobalt Soul credentials earned a private detour to a back vault with a fellow elephantine woman, a member of the Soul going back centuries.
- The archivists here spoke among themselves in **Panhiran**, not the city's common Qoniiran — a language most of the party didn't recognize.
- She showed them the **Orb of Avalir**: a self-shrinking arcane focus, its surface covered edge-to-edge in minuscule script. It once belonged to **Patia Porco**, Archmage of the flying city of Avalir and a member of a faction called the **Brass Ring** — sometimes blamed (unreliably, in Grimbold's estimation) for helping trigger the Calamity. Porco died instantly at the Calamity's start; her orb, containing an attempt to record **all written knowledge of Avalir**, was carried out by a man named **Serret**, who with his wife founded what would become the Cobalt Soul, right here in Niirdal-Poc — originally meant as oversight to prevent another Avalir-style abuse of magic.
- The orb is kept locked away and not for sale, "for a reason" left unspecified.
- **Grimcold used Eyes of the Bookworm** to search it for anything touching on the advanced arcane theory in the notes taken off the slain Nagpa — an Investigation check with advantage, cut short as the party was gently ushered out for standing too close. *(Unresolved — what, if anything, Grimcold found is not yet known.)*

#### The Crown Debate

The party's longest conversation of the session: what to do with the wizard-attunement mind-control crown looted from the Nagpa, which Jordana Whisk (Session 120) called "a very evil possession" she wouldn't buy, though Identify never flagged it as cursed. The **Crown of the Mindmaster Matriarch**.

- **Rowan** argued hardest against using it: a power that can only ever be wielded for evil will corrupt whoever holds it, however good their intentions.

> Rowan: "Sometimes we do the right thing because it's right, not because there's an award involved. Taking an evil item out of the world that can only ever be used to mind control others is the right thing in my view."

- **Tomis** pushed back, arguing mind control is no more inherently evil than the killing the party already does routinely, and drew a parallel to Rowan's own experience navigating the fey courts' nebulous morality.

> Tomis: "I see no more evil in this item than the bullets that fly from our guns. Mind control is not inherently evil — no more so than ending their life. If anything, one could argue that's even more severe."

- **Uriel** was pragmatic — the crown might have prevented a party death against the Aarakocra general whose sword nearly one-shot someone recently — and noted enchantment magic isn't inherently more suspect than anything else they use.
- **Freya** didn't object to Grimbold using it, but was explicit about the position it put her in:

> Freya: "He'd probably be able to use that well against our foes. But if something goes wrong, I feel like I'm just the one that's like — hey Freya, go chop off your friend's head because he's getting too persnickety with his evil shit now. And I don't want to be in that position."

- **Compromise:** Grimbold attunes to the crown for **two days**, on his word (and Grimcold's) that nothing untoward happens with it, and that it returns to shared party holding after — unless the party votes to extend. Grimcold's own attunement slots were already full (Eyes of the Bookworm, plus the Mageplate Cap he'd been wearing), so the crown went to Grimbold; Grimcold's freed-up Mageplate Cap stays with the party.

#### Attunements and Inventory

- Current attunements as reviewed at the table: **Grimbold** — Bell of Dormancy(?), an abjuration item heard as "Abjurer's Gilder," Robe of the Archmagi, now the Crown; **Grimcold** — Eyes of the Bookworm, Mageplate Cap (freed up); **Freya** — her weapon, griffin feather cape; **Uriel** — his weapon, a "Wooden Chain of the Unbroken Circle," a ring/periapt heard as "Parry Out of Reflection"; **Tomis** — Stormkeeper, Spellguard Shield, a "Shadow Ward"; **Rowan** — his armor, weapon, and a ring cut off mid-list.
- The party kept the **Baton of Many Sizes** rather than sell it — cheap, no attunement slot, and it's bailed them out before (climbing/anchoring during the fight on the plane of Mechanus).
- **Rowan handed Gregan's Ring** (casts a 1st/2nd-level spell as a bonus action) to Grimcold, since he wasn't using it himself.

#### Supply Run in Niirdal-Teek

- The party deposited the bulk of their Whitestone reward goods (~90,000 gp) with the auction house for appraisal, keeping roughly 10,000 gp in hand.
- Grimbold flew the party (5th-level Fly, Rowan and Grimbold doubling up on Juniper) toward the university district — **Niirdal-Teek** — to a supply shop serving students, run by an old friend and former professor, an aging rabbitfolk (Harengon) named **Basque Tellin**.
- Warm reunion — the professor gave the party one item free (~150 gp value) in exchange for travel stories, including Grimbold's account of his time on the plane of Mechanus.
- Purchases: **2 Heroes' Feast gem-encrusted bowls**, more diamonds and diamond dust (bringing totals to roughly **800 gp diamonds, 700 gp diamond dust, 150 gp ruby dust, 1,500 gp incense** — mostly Tomis restocking Revivify and higher-tier material components), plus **Identify** and **Find Familiar** scrolls and a spree of first/second-level spell scrolls for Grimbold to transcribe. The party briefly bit on whether spellbooks have a real page limit and never got a satisfying answer.

#### Commune with the Matron

With the day running late and roughly half a day of buffer left before they wanted to be back in Vasselheim, **Tomis cast Commune directly** (no intermediary this time) and asked:

1. *Is the Nagpa's true objective something inside the city of Vasselheim?* → Leaning yes, but uncertain — the Matron isn't fully in the know at present.
2. *Would taking back control of Emon be a good miracle to attempt?* → Tentative yes.
3. *Should the party stop by the Hexahedron of Sarqet* (a shrine in the city of **Niirdal-Sarqet** where each face bears a prime deity)? → No clear signal either way.

The party also weighed and shelved buying into a **Planar Binding + Magic Circle** setup to try to trap and recruit a celestial ally — appealing in theory, but no immediate plan to execute it.
