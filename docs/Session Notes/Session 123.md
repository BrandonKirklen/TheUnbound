### Previously On: The Unbound

At the homecoming party in Niirdal-Poc, Tomis heard Dugan mention a half-elf named **Leona**, sister of Aei'Tu, traveling with another adventuring party. The name broke something loose that had been buried deep in his mind, a potent magic very few could hope to overcome. The edges of his memories of Leona no longer fit. He brought it to Rowan, who listened, and was deeply concerned for him.

After the party, rather than teleporting straight into Vasselheim again, the Unbound teleported to the base of the city and made the customary pilgrimage up the **Thousand Steps**. The city was on full war footing against the two flying cities bearing down on it, and the remains of the **Titan** that Vecna once brought here were being torn apart, out of fear of what the new death god might do with them. What that means for the Matron of Ravens, whose hiding place it once was, went unasked.

Grimbold then cast **Demiplane** inside the **Raven's Crest Temple** to prepare Glyphs of Warding. Two **Adjudicators** found the party almost at once. Rowan kept it from becoming a fight and Grimbold went with them, until he tried to slip away and was beaten into paralysis. **Grimcold** spent his only Teleport to pull them both out to the base of the Thousand Steps. The rest of the party watched the two of them shimmer and vanish, and the Adjudicators turned to look at them.

### Session Notes

#### The Arrest

- The Adjudicators made clear, with no insight check needed, that they intended to take the rest of the party in.
  - Rowan put his hands up: "I'll go willingly."
  - Uriel judged anything but compliance pointless. Tomis didn't want to provoke them further.
- They stripped everything overtly magical from the party: Rowan's light armor (from the Seelie Court) and rings, and Tomis's gear, **including his horseshoes**. They are magical and came off without a blacksmith.
- Tomis tried to calm them: "Sure, sure, calm down, guys. We will go with you, there's no need for this." (Persuasion at disadvantage, DC 30.) They didn't respond, and said very little throughout.
- Tomis called loudly for help. Insight (19): every priest and lower cleric who saw what was happening showed real fear. Adjudicators are deployed only in the most severe circumstances. Most people know them from legend, and nobody stayed to watch.
- Each prisoner was fitted with a non-magical, strapped-on **muzzle** that blocked verbal components. As they went to muzzle him, Rowan said:

> Rowan: "There's no need for that. You have our vow that we will not try anything."

- Persuasion at disadvantage, DC 30: a natural 1.
- Freya, at DC 25 with disadvantage: "I ain't no fucking arcanist!" It failed. The party had burned all goodwill with these two.
- While one Adjudicator processed the party, the other cast a **divination ritual** over the runes left by the teleport, tracing where Grimbold and Grimcold had gone.
- The party's telepathic bond wasn't active, so they couldn't talk privately. Before the muzzles went on, Rowan said: "Time to go to jail, I suppose. I'll wait a few, maybe a few decades. They'll release me eventually." He was openly penitent, aware of how close this came to one of his own vows.
- Freya: "You know, this was not my first time getting arrested right before an enemy attacked. They let you go if you tell them enough that you're gonna fight for them instead. Enemy of my enemy."
- Rowan used his mental link to tell **Juniper** to lie low at the Raven's Crest.

#### The Procession to the Platinum Sanctuary

- The party was shackled in a chain gang, Tomis's four legs included, and marched slowly uphill. The whole procession took about 45 minutes.
- In the streets, people didn't gawk. They ducked into buildings and turned on their heels. Among the soldiers drilling on every terrace up to the **Platinum Sanctuary** there was open hostility, since anyone brought in like this must be a bad actor, a day before those soldiers expected to fight for their lives.
- Vasselheim's rules on arcane magic are draconian, but people get away with small castings. There is so much superstition about what the Adjudicators can perceive that no one wants to cross them.
- The crash site of **Whitestone's Honor** has been fully cleaned up.
- Uriel spotted **Belithos** drilling a regiment. He clocked what was happening, handed off his soldiers, and headed up another stairway.
- The war room was in session, its doors open and loud arguing audible. The party was led away from the ornate guest areas into the plain military wing, then into a holding area under an **antimagic field**. Everyone but Freya felt their connection to the Weave or the divine go quiet.
- The cells were tiny, about 5 feet to pace, with a cot, a bucket, a heavy metal door with a small barred port, and walls of single seamless stone. No one could see into another cell. Three cells were already occupied. Shackles and muzzles came off at the cell doors.

#### Grimbold and Grimcold on the Run

- The pair arrived beaten and bruised at the base of the Thousand Steps: a waystation with a tavern, a large stable for travelers heading into Issylra, and a few outbuildings, but few residences.
- Grimbold hid roughly a thousand feet away. Investigation with advantage (22) found a small bush den around a bend in the road, with a sightline to the Steps. He restored his wards and began a short rest. He has nothing that blocks divination.
- Before the rest finished, Grimcold tapped his shoulder. Two regular city guards had come out of the tavern, someone was pointing down the path after him, and they started walking his way.
- Grimbold cast **Fly** on both of them, skimmed the canopy for about a mile (Stealth 15 and 9), and ritually cast **Phantom Steed** over 11 minutes. He rode the road for two miles, then turned off into the woods and rode for about an hour. Survival 12 meant scraped legs and less than top speed.
- He had no way to reach the party. He hadn't prepared Sending, and he and Grimcold hadn't used the crown to set up a telepathic bond with the others. He planned to wait for dark and sneak back toward the city (Survival to time it well), and took another short rest while keeping watch (Perception 6).
- About an hour and a half later, a large winged serpent came down into his clearing with an **Adjudicator** on its back. The rider looked straight at his hiding spot.

> Grimbold: "Hey, we're outside your jurisdiction, buddy. You better stop right there."
>
> Adjudicator: "My jurisdiction is boundless."

- Grimbold cast Fly on himself and Grimcold again, but the mount was faster. A telepathic voice, not the rider's, said: "Cease your flight."
- Grimbold: "Hey, you have one chance to land yourself, buddy, or I'm gonna ground you." The course didn't change.
- He cast **Vortex Warp** on the mount, which failed its Constitution save and was teleported out from under its rider. As he fell, the rider fired a blast from his gauntlet (25 to hit; Shield wouldn't stop it). The damage was small, but it **branded** Grimbold with a low, glowing shimmer and badly depleted his ward. The mount caught its rider in under half a round and resumed the chase.
- Arcana (21): the mount is a **couatl**, a powerful celestial in its own right with strong divination magic. The rider never scanned for him. He flew straight to Grimbold's position.
- Unwilling to fight the city's own defenders ("it would not be prudent to anger our allies"), Grimbold broke line of sight under the canopy and **teleported** himself and Grimcold to **Whitestone's** teleportation circle with his linked token. No roll was needed.
- It was the middle of the night in Whitestone. Nobody questioned a familiar face. He took his usual guest suite in the castle, put up **Tiny Hut** and plenty of alarms, and slept on what may be the opposite side of the globe from the party. The brand faded after about an hour.

#### The Cells

- Uriel entered his **Hearing the Air** stance (blindsight 30 ft). Perception 32: he could hear the three other prisoners breathing, awake and silent.
- Freya spent the whole time doing push-ups. Tomis had "hardly even been in cities with more than 10 people in them, let alone in prison."
- Rowan offered a story. An elderly woman's voice answered: "I wouldn't mind a tale." He told of his old party's war against **Evron the Rime Lord** two centuries ago, including a stint in jail in Westruun over a disputed treasure ("Old Strongbreaker really wanted that hat"). Performance 18.
- Tomis tested for a Zone of Truth by telling a lie: "God, I hate Grimbold." The words came out freely.
- Uriel introduced himself. After a long pause the woman answered: "Oh, don't let them be rude. I'm **Vaelora**." Tomis's History checks (16, then 2 on a DC 18 re-roll) turned up nothing.

> Tomis: "We are the adventuring party, the Unbound, or the Fated Few, depending on who you are."
>
> Vaelora: "You seem a bit bound."

- Asked why she was here, Vaelora said "Nothing, really." Insight: there was a smirk in it, as if it were wordplay rather than a denial. Tomis's thoughts went to a nullification field.
- She is **formerly Sister Vaelora**, a religious title in the Matron of Ravens' order. She guessed from Tomis's accent that he wasn't from Vasselheim: "I suppose it makes sense that they don't talk about me anymore."

> Vaelora: "What do you imagine happened when the first mortal cast the first magical spell? What do you think the others thought of it?"
>
> Tomis: "Probably a mix of fear, revulsion, as well as awe, and perhaps jealousy. And for the ones that are able to put down their own inadequacy, a desire to learn."
>
> Vaelora: "I think it's fair to say people fear that which they lack understanding of."
>
> Tomis: "Fear is what drove me to the Matron in the first place. Fear of what comes after."

#### Lieve'tel's First Visit

- A visibly exasperated **Lieve'tel** entered the prison.

> Lieve'tel: "What the fuck did you all do?"

- Rowan explained the demiplane and glyph plan. Lieve'tel: "And no one stopped at any point? Because now I'm having to stop them from sending out a skyship to hunt him down."
- Rowan: "We were frankly stupid in that moment, and no, we did not stop him. It was a lapse in judgment."
- Freya explained that she and Uriel had been walking away, saw the guards running back the way they'd come, followed, and were rounded up with everyone nearby.
- Tomis admitted he hadn't thought about how that kind of planar magic would read here, since he'd been using Plane Shift lately, and that Grimbold flipping out and trying to escape had put everyone on edge.

> Lieve'tel: "I must now exit the primary conversations being had to go find a way to put this right. Less even for the freedom of you all, and more so for the already dismal reputation of my temple. So thank you."
>
> Tomis: "I have no words but an apology. I'm so sorry, Lieve'tel. I know this is costing you both socially, as well as in our own standing. I am ashamed."

- Insight: she isn't just angry. This is costing her something specific. She left muttering under her breath.

#### Vaelora and Korath

- Vaelora knew of Lieve'tel and served at the Raven's Crest "just before she was the Dawn Marshal." She has been here "not as long as **Korath**."
- Asked what Korath had done, a man's voice answered:

> Korath: "The city of the gods is not yet prepared for the bell beneath the world."
>
> Tomis: "The bell beneath the world. Can't help but think of a dismal place at the bottom of the ocean."
>
> Korath: "Cough and laugh if you wish. It is your doom that it prophesies."

- Korath had seen the Forgotten One's vision "and more" when he tried to warn the city, and that is why he was locked up. Vaelora: "He's been in here longer than the rest of us."
- Insight (24): Tomis is starting to read Vaelora's tells. She was smiling.

> Tomis: "Vaelora, did you know the Forgotten One?"
>
> Vaelora: (chuckling) "Do you not recognize the paradoxical nature of your question?"

- Tomis asked why she left the order. "I figured some things out." What? "Something you'll learn. Or perhaps you'll die." Has she died? "A complicated question."
- Tomis spoke about resurrection: he isn't sure how far the dead have gone when he pulls them back, or whether time flows the same beyond the veil.

> Vaelora: "And those you bring back, do you think them whole?"
>
> Tomis: "First one definitely is not. Not very happy with me. Keeps fucking up my life. Uriel seems well here, at least. A little less whole, though. I feel like it's often just that I have more use of them, and so I still need them here. Pretty selfish."

- Freya said coming back leaves you mostly intact, but "you kind of lose a bit of yourself every time." Vaelora: "Well, perhaps you've never truly done it before." Tomis blurted out that Freya has the **glyphs of Asmodeus** inscribed on her back, which may be keeping her from ever truly dying.
- Tomis asked whether Vaelora knew of the **Dusters**. She didn't. He told her of the Inevitable that had masqueraded as one, seeking the one final death. Vaelora: "I imagine his search came up empty-handed." Tomis: "I think it was four-handed, actually." Freya: "This search was pretty successful, actually."
- Asked what Tomis doesn't know about his own god, Vaelora turned it around: what does he know? Tomis described the Matron as a warmer version of an afterlife, one who doesn't pull on the lines of fate to cut them, unlike the Forgotten One, who "relished in the act rather than was a nurturing force for it."

> Vaelora: "Does it not strike you as ridiculous, this constant reference to the Forgotten One? Perhaps you should just call him **the First**."
>
> Freya: "Nah, I'd rather call him the loser."

- That set Tomis wondering whether there was a generation of gods before this one. Rowan had heard nothing. As far as any recorded history goes, there is no pantheon before the current one, except the idea that the Raven Queen supplanted someone when she ascended.
- Freya brought up the primordials and the Titan. Korath: "Was?" Tomis told him it is being dismantled.

> Korath: "You all seem to think that that's the end of the story. It's just the first domino to fall. The bell beneath the world still waits."

- Tomis's History roll on "the bell" came up a natural 1. To him it sounds absurd, a man-made object cast as a primordial evil, and not the product of a sage mind. His Insight on Korath's conviction was also a natural 1, so he can't tell whether Korath believes it or is telling them to go away.
- Freya asked for the scariest thing anyone had done. Rowan said he fought an ancient white dragon, then fought it again in his own mind where no one could help him. Freya said she lived through the Calamity. Korath: "I've seen the world unmade countless times." Vaelora: "Oh, no. I prefer not to think of that sort of thing."
- The party tried to rest. Korath woke them screaming about the bell beneath the world and how he had watched them all die a thousand deaths. Vaelora: "Well, you'll get used to it." No one finished a long rest.

#### The Interrogation and Release

> Lieve'tel: "Tomis, I'd prefer to not have to do this in a Zone of Truth. So please, just tell it plain. What did Grimbold do? Where did he go? What has he told you?"

- Tomis laid it out: the pocket dimension, the glyphs and trigger words, the party being pulled out, the scuffle, and Grimcold teleporting them both away.
- A second voice in the room, recognized by Tomis (who had met him alongside Grimbold), was the **High Archivist**: "The accounts match. All of the evidence found…" He seemed to be backing Lieve'tel to an unseen third party.
- Lieve'tel asked whether Grimbold had contacted them since. "No, not that I know of." "Do you swear that on the name of the Matron?" "I swear that on the name of the Matron."
- A scoff came from Vaelora's cell.

> Lieve'tel: "Are we done here? They came here to protect this city, not attack it."

- An unfamiliar voice: "Fine, they can go. Return their things."
- The party was released that evening. Their belongings came back with no apology. Belithos oversaw it: "Make sure they get everything." To Rowan's thanks: "Do better."
- Lieve'tel wasn't there, and the party sensed she was the last person who wanted to see them. Another incident like this would likely damage their relationship with her permanently.

#### The Night at the Gilded Griffin

- Tomis sent Grimbold a **Sending**: "Hey, you stupid fuck. We're out of prison now. Thanks a lot." He ended it by telling him the High Archivist had vouched for him and to talk to him. There was no reply, and no sense the spell had reached anyone. Grimbold was asleep in his Tiny Hut.

> Freya: "He's gonna be here tomorrow for the fight or he ain't, and that's on him at this point."
>
> Tomis: "I have a sneaking suspicion we're never gonna see him again."
>
> Rowan: "A party member who flees their party at the first sign of danger and abandons them to their fate. Is it really a party member we want to have?"

- The party agreed on a lesson going forward:

> Tomis: "I think our objective is to make Lieve'tel not mad at us anymore."
>
> Freya: "Let's do that by being effective."
>
> Rowan: "We should at least pay closer attention to the way our actions are reflected in local laws."
>
> Freya: "Try to respect local laws. That's a very attainable goal. I support this."
>
> Rowan: "You're not the only one that feels bad about this one, but sometimes oversights happen. Best we can do is take the beating, take the lesson learned, move forward."

- Rather than show their faces at the temple, they stayed at the **Gilded Griffin** (1 gp for the lot). The party was nearly 24 hours without sleep and had items to re-attune. The adventurers' tavern was boisterous: bravado and old war stories pushed down the dread of the coming day, and no one was sure they'd have another night. Tomis joined in a little. Rowan followed his lead, though underneath he felt like shit about himself.

#### The Morning

- In Whitestone, Grimbold prepared spells, then used a 4th-level slot to send Rowan a letter by magical courier: was it safe to teleport back for the battle without being attacked by Adjudicators? The courier spell can't carry a reply. Rowan, meditating at the Gilded Griffin, decided it wasn't worth waking Tomis. Grimbold ate breakfast with the castle staff and spent a couple of hours scribing spells.
- The party slept late. Rowan woke Tomis with the letter.
- The debate:
  - Freya was sure Grimbold would be arrested if he came back. Rowan doubted the city would spare forces for it with an attack hours away.
  - Grimbold cannot teleport into the city. Rowan proposed meeting him at the bottom of the Thousand Steps with Juniper.
  - Tomis floated having Grimbold meet them at the incoming cities instead. Rowan objected that a few miles off course "is the difference of life or death once the battle starts."
  - There are no Whitestone airships here. Its fleet is still recovering from the fight at the League of the Miracle Base. The defending fleet is mostly Marquesian, plus banners they didn't recognize.
  - The adventurers in the tavern had talked only of ground defense, with old greybeards recalling the Vecna cultist attack. No one is planning an offensive. That had been Lieve'tel's own radical proposal, and "the defense of the city is the defense of the city." Tomis wondered if that is what she gave up for them.
  - Rowan floated reaching out to **Pike**, "someone we haven't broken our trust with in the last 24 hours, probably."
- Tomis sent to Lieve'tel: "Good morning. Best of luck to you today. Should I tell Grimbold to come and join the defense? And if so, how can we help best?" Protective magic **blocked** the Sending.
- Tomis sent to Grimbold: "Hey Grimbold, you fuck. We would really appreciate it if you came back. We don't know whether or not you're fully allowed in the city. We've been released from jail. Rowan will meet you at the bottom of the Thousand Steps."
- Grimbold decided to finish breakfast first and then teleport over. Rowan ate fast and called for Juniper to fly him down.
- An odd hush fell over the tavern's breakfast. Someone asked if they'd been outside: news barkers were calling it all over the city. **The cities had stopped.** The attack was postponed or delayed, and none was expected immediately.
- Looking east, the party could see **two large black specks** fixed on the horizon, not close but unmistakable.

**The session ended here.**
