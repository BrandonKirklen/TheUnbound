# Session 116

**Date:** 13th of Fessuran
**Days Remaining Until Freya (7th of Quen'pillar):** 23

---

### Recap

After Tomis and Grimbold's mission to return Kobach Alanis ended in disaster — Tomis ambushed and taken by what now appear to be Myriad agents, Grimbold leaping through a portal after him, Kobach lost in the rescue — the party gathered themselves for the larger work in front of them. The Raven Queen counseled against direct retaliation against the Myriad. Frustrated and grieving, the party redirected south.

Their target: the **Alabaster Sierras**, where Aarakocra have organized into a coordinated presence and where intelligence places a **Nagpa** — the same shadowy, illusion-cloaked figure that chased the party through the castle of the League of Miracles, now apparently tasked with dealing with *"the upstarts of Tal'Dorei."* Gray Warden reports confirmed dozens to a hundred individuals concentrated in the southern peaks, with raids harrying caravans on the Whitestone–Turstfields road and the river run essentially shut down.

The party departed Whitestone aboard *the Shale*, captained by Jethro, traveling downriver under cover of magic and discipline. A daylight Aarakocra scout was spotted pacing the boat. Uriel and Juniper intercepted it in a high-stakes aerial chase ending with the scout dropping a net on Juniper, both rider and pegasus going into freefall, Uriel teleporting to engage, Juniper breaking the net and catching him out of the air. The scout went down. Tomis tried Speak with Dead on the corpse and got nothing useful but threats — *"You will die, you will die, you will die."* He threw the body in the river.

Reconnaissance turned up abandoned surveillance platforms along the river bends, a recurring **glint near the southernmost summit**, and from four hours out a 32 Perception revealed **dark figures across multiple peaks** — a settlement, not a patrol. The party disembarked on the eastern shore of the Parchwood Timberlands. Uriel cast Pass Without Trace. Tomis raised the storm — *"Let the mountain wear its shroud. What moves beneath is of no concern to these sky creatures."* The session closed with the party at the foot of the climb, fog rolling in.

---

### Up the Lower Slopes

The party began the ascent the old-fashioned way. Grimbold pulled what he could from his general knowledge of mountain country (Nature 14 — workable, not commanding). Tomis took the lead with thirty years of experience hunting these specific foothills (Survival 24), pulling on tribal memory of goat trails the herders used before the Aarakocra drove them off. Between the two of them the party made expeditious progress through the lower forested band, the fog clinging low and thick despite the midday sun making it obvious the weather was magical in origin.

Marching order settled with Tomis leading on his hooves, Grimbold riding behind him, Freya on the phantom warg, Rowan and Uriel on Juniper. Pass Without Trace held its hour. The fog held its five-mile radius.

About two and a half hours in, the terrain steepened. The mountain stopped offering paths and started demanding climbs. The goat trails thinned out, the gullies forced detours that lost them elevation, and the calculation tipped: continuing on foot would either eat too much time or commit them to a vertical ascent with rope and pitons — slow, exposed, and likely to be spotted once they broke the treeline.

### The Magical Reroute

The party reconsidered. A 5th-level Fly cast on three party members plus Juniper carrying the rest could cover the remaining 2,800-ish vertical feet in a fraction of the spell's duration. Grimbold also took the time to cast **Telepathic Bond** across the group. The plan: hug the mountain, fly at half speed for stealth, ride the fog up to the summit cluster.

Group Stealth check landed at a 25 average even without Pass Without Trace boosting it. The party made the climb cleanly. Along the way Uriel and other high-passive-perception members caught glimpses of small posted scout stations cut into the rock — supplies, weapons, but unmanned in this weather — and frequent thatched-over cave entrances built into the natural fissures of the upper mountain. Dwellings, in the rock itself.

Tomis had also kicked off a weather change as they began the magical ascent: from moderate wind to **strong wind**, twenty minutes out.

### The Summit

The fog began to thin near the cap as the rising wind started to do what wind does. The party slowed, hugged the rock, landed at intervals to stay out of the open air, and crested into view of the **Aarakocra settlement** — a crown of dwellings along the tops of four or five peaks in the southernmost cluster, built at angles only flying creatures could survive, no bridges between summits, terraces carved at the highest points, the rest of the construction in harmony with the natural mountain face. Open thatched doorways closing one by one as the storm rolled in. Constant aerial traffic between peaks. Most of the Aarakocra they could see were armed — footbows slung on backs.

On the **highest peak** sat the source of the river-glint: **an old observatory**, dramatically older than the Aarakocra settlement, its dome punctured, its facade stripped for building material, its **telescope still jutting through the broken roof**. Around its base, the mountain had been **terraformed into broad layered perches** — meaning whoever built the observatory came up here on legs, meaning there was a path. Tomis took it in and spoke into the bond: *"These thieves nest in what builders once made. It is the only thing they know how to do."*

### Finding the Path

With the fog actively receding and time tight, the party fanned out in the air to find the terrestrial approach the observatory's terraforming implied. Uriel watched for cut and modified stone (Perception). Tomis read the wind currents — feathers tracing eddies across the rock face — for the topography underneath (Survival 29). Rowan drew on a lifetime of past observatory visits, particularly time spent in **Luthodern**, to reason about how such structures get supplied (History).

The clues converged at an average of 23.6. They found it: **a dwarven staircase**, switchbacking up the back face of the highest peak, carved narrow for dwarven proportions and built so well it had survived long disuse without maintenance. The same kind of work as the approach to **Kraghammer**. The staircase was on the **opposite face from the central settlement cluster** — naturally obscured from the Aarakocra's daily aerial traffic. Excellent cover for an approach on foot.

### The Nagpa

The party landed on the staircase, hunkered into an alcove, and held position to let the weather complete its shift. Tomis kicked the precipitation up to **hail** to cut visibility. The plan was to wait, then climb under storm cover.

Then Freya knocked some rocks loose and got shoved into a cove by Uriel. A group Stealth roll came in catastrophically low — a 6 average. Perception checks went around as they hunkered. About **a hundred to a hundred and fifty feet above them**, silhouetted briefly against the storm-dark summit near the observatory, **four or five figures** appeared at the ledge, peered down, and retreated.

To everyone else they were figures. **To Freya, who had seen this exact silhouette pattern before — illusory shadow-clones flanking a single body — it was the Nagpa.** The same one that had chased her through the League of Miracles castle.

Whether it had spotted the party was unreadable. There was no urgency in the movement, but no certainty either way. Uriel attempted to mark it as quarry; the mark failed because he had targeted one of the illusory clones, not the body. He cast See Invisibility and confirmed the trick.

A brief panic-debate over whether to attack immediately resolved on a 20 Intelligence check from Grimbold: if the Nagpa had wanted them dead, this was the moment — strapped to a precarious staircase, most of them grounded, nowhere to go. The fact that nothing was happening to them right now meant they probably hadn't been seen, or at minimum hadn't been judged worth acting on.

Uriel then used **Extraplanar Awareness** and confirmed something significant: a **portal to the Plane of Air**, inside the observatory, **held open by some sort of magic**. That changed the stakes. Closing the portal became a stated objective.

### Final Preparations

The party committed to waiting out the next weather tick. Tomis kicked off **gale-force winds** — which once they take effect will make non-magical flight nearly impossible and ground the entire Aarakocra settlement, but which also lock the weather in for forty minutes once they take hold (Tomis can't reverse a stage until it has fully set). Tomis distributed **Aid** at 3rd level to Rowan, Juniper, and Uriel. Freya already has **Death Ward** running. Grimbold considered the Tiny Hut and then declined to spend the cast time.

Marching order for the climb: **Uriel** in front, **Freya on Juniper** second, **Rowan** third, **Tomis** fourth (centered for protection), **Grimbold** rear (tanky, the only one for whom the staircase is a comfortable fit).

---

### The Sentry

Uriel crested a half-turn of the staircase — and met the eyes of an **Aarakocra sentry** sheltering from the rain under the next shelf. The sentry shrieked. The cry carried into the storm but the gale dampened how far. Initiative.

Uriel sprinted, Arvanhon firing point-blank for 19 damage, marked it as quarry, swung twice in melee. The sentry survived the round, its shriek concluding.

Rowan brought Juniper double-moving up the stairs carrying Freya into melee, teleported 30 feet up himself, cast **Crusader's Mantle** to light up Uriel and Freya with bonus radiant.

The sentry tried to disengage. **Sentinel reaction.** Freya's great axe came down with full Crusader's Mantle and full hatred — **62 damage** — and the kill triggered something the table had only seen once before. The blood drained into the axe. The body shriveled, exsanguinated, became a husk. From Freya's back, **two Aarakocra wings sprouted.** Fly speed 45.

No visible response from the observatory. Combat dropped. The party reformed and began the final push toward the dome.

---

### The Storm of the Castle

Cresting the penultimate stair, Freya saw what was waiting. **Five identical shadowy Nagpa figures** on the ledge above. Twenty feet to their left, an **Aarakocra military commander** — well-armored, the bearing of an officer — flanked by archers, with several more scattered around the observatory grounds. Tomis with a 20 passive insight caught what the others missed: under the strain, the commander hid it well, but the archers' faces showed **significant worry**, and every one of them was **grounded**.

The Nagpa: *"I'd wondered how quickly you'd seek your demise."* The mountain began to shake.

### The Tenth-Level Spell

Grimbold rolled a 28 Arcana to identify the spell — and recognized it. **Tenth-level magic.** Beyond modern arcane understanding to counter. He cast Counterspell anyway. 19. No effect. He filed it away — *if anyone would one day figure out how to make this possible, it would be an abjurer who survived a fight with a creature casting spells of this magnitude.*

The Nagpa: *"There is none more destructive than that which dwells within. I need not come up with the fears, for you have those of your own making."*

Wisdom save DC 23. Rowan, Tomis, and Grimbold made it. Freya burned an exploit die to convert her failure. **Uriel failed.** Rowan used **Divine Vigilance** to force a re-roll — Uriel made it.

But the rerolls don't change what happens. They change the *consequences* of what happens. The blackness coalesced anyway. Each party member saw their own private nightmare — visible only to themselves, but able to hurt them:

- **Uriel:** the Dao that took his father.
- **Rowan:** the **ancient white dragon Rhime'Sor**, descending out of the storm.
- **Freya:** **Vespin Chloras himself** — gaunt and unhealthy, the man responsible for her curse, founder of the Calamity, with **Primrose** his companion at his side.
- **Tomis:** the Aarakocra. Not just any one. The one with the bone-white notch in her lower beak, where his spear caught her on the upswing the morning the herd was scattered. The one that took El-li.

### Vespin's Charm

Vespin pointed at Freya: *"Your heart already belongs to another. Why don't you join the winning side again?"* DC 23 Charisma save. Freya's strongest save. She rolled high and burned a second exploit die to lock it. **She held.**

### The Counter-Push

**Tomis** cast **Spiritual Sundering** — Charisma-save AoE catching the Nagpa cluster plus both archers. The archers ate full damage. The Nagpa took half but no debuff.

**Grimbold** ducked behind cover where his stature made him invisible to Primrose, hopped on a fresh **Bigby's Hand**, dismounted onto the ledge, and grappled Primrose with advantage (28 Athletics — Primrose burned a Legendary Resistance trying to escape). He plunged her **off the cliff** and held her down to drown in a small storm-filled basin below. *"Stay at the pond. Stay."*

**Uriel** self-cast **Haste** and closed on the Nagpa cluster — wrong target.

**Rowan's white dragon** unleashed a breath weapon. Both Rowan and Juniper failed even with advantage. **81 cold damage**, no Absorb Elements available (he'd burned the reaction on Uriel's save).

**Vespin** cast at Uriel — Wisdom save, Periapt of Reflection reroll, **success**. The figure burrowed underground to close instead.

**Primrose** snapped her fingers — **Contingency** activating. Grimbold counterspelled at 32 vs. her DC. **Countered.**

### The Recognition

Tomis saw her on the ledge. The notch in her lower beak, visible from forty feet through hail. The pale ridge. The angle of her wing. Thirty years of hunting, ten thousand, nine hundred and fifty nights of picturing her, and here she was.

He took a breath. Pushed it down — not forever, just for the moment. Turned to his friends. Muttered: *"You can wait."*

Then cast **Firestorm**. The roof of the observatory burst into flame. Critical secondary effect: the **burning illusion clones revealed which one was actually being damaged** — making the real Nagpa visible at last. One archer burned to a crisp. The Nagpa took partial damage and now had a target painted on it.

Tomis Athletics-jumped over the Aarakocra commander blocking the stair (provoked, took 15) to reposition.

### The Magic Missile Solution

**Grimbold** had an epiphany. With a 30 Arcana, he sensed the Nagpa was **maintaining Concentration** on the nightmare spell. He fired off **Magic Missile** — *can't miss* — with the explicit understanding that Magic Missile would continue to seek the real target across each clone-swap. Future missiles would re-reveal the Nagpa's position immediately after each swap, neutralizing the trick.

### Uriel's Gunnado

**Uriel** activated his **Blooster Schoofin** technique — calling the gale-force winds Tomis had been generating into a **40-foot radius, 100-foot tall cylindrical storm** around himself. Six shotgun shots loaded into the storm itself. Lightning-channeled blade.

First attack: target the rightmost (real) Nagpa. The Nagpa burned its **reaction-swap**. After that, all gloves off.

Second attack: lightning channeled down through the blade — **Dex save**, the Nagpa failed (rolled 14, DC presumably high). **68 lightning damage.** Then two more shotgun strikes from the storm — 25 and 24 damage, with knockback. The Nagpa was knocked **10 feet per shot toward the cliff edge.**

Three Concentration saves required: DC 18, DC 12, DC 18. The Nagpa was rolling at +10. **First save: Legendary Resistance burned.** Second: passed. Third: passed. Concentration on the nightmare spell **held**, but the Nagpa was now bloodied, prone at the edge of the roof, and visible.

### The Dragon Returns

**Rhime'Sor** the ancient white dragon descended on the paralyzed Rowan. Two claw attacks, both crits (paralyzed = auto-crit on hit in melee). Rowan hit zero, **Death Ward triggered**. Then the maul-strike from a **Dao** appeared at the ledge to hit Uriel for 19. Uriel reaction-teleported into the observatory using a 4th-level slot, gaining resistance, taking 9.

### Vespin Power Word Pain

**Vespin Chloras** cast Power Word Pain on Freya. *"This is the spell I used to torture you for years."* Freya's saves now at disadvantage on top of everything else.

**Pit Fiend bite.** Constitution save. Freya failed by 6. **Poisoned.** Cannot regain hit points. **66 poison damage at the start of every turn.** End-of-turn re-save permitted.

Freya provoked from Vespin to fly into melee with the Nagpa and **blood-smited** for 70 damage — the Nagpa burned its **second and final** Legendary Resistance to maintain Concentration (DC 35 was impossible for it to roll at +10).

### Tomis Goes Down

Two Aarakocra warlords closed on Tomis — the real one and the illusion-version (only visible to Tomis). Four attacks, two crits, **62 damage**. War Caster advantage on Concentration saves let him hold. Then more archer fire — Shadow Ward charge spent to teleport and halve incoming.

Rhime'Sor breathed cold again. Tomis went down. The illusion Aarakocra warlord finished him.

### Vespin Disintegrates Freya

Vespin's turn. *"Now that we're all here, shall we begin?"* DC 24 Dexterity save. Freya rolled — **failed**.

**Disintegrate.** Freya at zero, can't be healed, no replacement effects available. The spell wording: *the target is disintegrated if this damage leaves it with zero hit points.*

Freya was reduced to dust on the ledge.

### The Amber Vault

Then — the Amber Vault Freya carried activated. Her own fortune-token, set long ago to predict her death. *"If the fortune correctly predicts your death, it becomes a scroll of revivify."* Material cost paid up front when scribed. The scroll was just fire-and-forget.

A scroll of Revivify, on Freya's body, after Disintegrate. Useless directly — Disintegrate requires True Resurrection or Wish. But it crystallized the moment.

### State at Session End

The Nagpa: bloodied, prone at the cliff edge, both Legendary Resistances spent, Concentration miraculously holding, real target now visible to all.

Tomis: **down**.
Rowan: **down**, paralyzed, Death Ward broken, sustained by Tomis's healing earlier and now back at zero from the dragon.
Freya: **disintegrated**.
Juniper: **down**.
Uriel: standing, in the observatory, Haste running, looking for the real Nagpa among clones again (knowledge resets per turn for Magic Missile tracking).
Grimbold: standing, maintaining the Magic Missile barrage, holding Primrose drowning in the basin below.

The Aarakocra commander, three archers, the Pit Fiend, Vespin Chloras, the Dao, and the white dragon all still active.
