Overhear some of the troops want to be back at Zarfell because they are trying to impress him. Pretty advanced language skills. In an attempt to sneak through, we attacked the guard post. The party was then chased back to the river valley where we were able to make camp, hopefully away from the watch parties. Tomis was able to chart a path back while also aiding Aei'Tu and Urial in their endeavors.
